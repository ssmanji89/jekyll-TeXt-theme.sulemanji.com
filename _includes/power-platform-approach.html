1

Discovery & Assessment

I begin by thoroughly analyzing your existing processes, understanding pain points, and identifying automation opportunities. This phase includes stakeholder interviews, process mapping, and baseline metric establishment.

2

Solution Design

Based on the assessment, I design a comprehensive automation solution that leverages the appropriate Power Platform components. This includes workflow mapping, data structure planning, and user experience design.

3

Development & Testing

I build the solution components, including Power Automate flows, SharePoint structures, Power Apps, and any necessary integrations. Rigorous testing ensures everything works flawlessly before deployment.

4

Deployment & Training

The solution is deployed to your production environment with careful change management. I provide comprehensive training to ensure your team can effectively use and maintain the new automated processes.

5

Optimization & Support

After implementation, I continue to monitor the solution's performance, make refinements based on user feedback, and provide ongoing support to ensure long-term success.
