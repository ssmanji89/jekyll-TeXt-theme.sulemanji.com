The Challenge

ZG Companies manages 23+ residential properties across multiple markets. Their portfolio requires regular property inspections to maintain quality standards and comply with regulatory requirements.

Before automation, ZG Companies faced several critical challenges with their property inspection process:

Time-Consuming Manual Workflow

Property managers spent 5-7 hours per inspection completing paperwork, manually transferring data, and generating reports.

Documentation Inconsistency

Different property managers used varying formats and standards for documentation, creating inconsistencies in reporting.

Compliance Risk

Inconsistent documentation increased the risk of non-compliance with HUD regulations during audits.

Delayed Reporting

Manual processes resulted in a 2-3 week lag between inspection and final report generation, delaying necessary maintenance actions.
